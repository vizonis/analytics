Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free resume templates

Are these resume templates really free?
Yes, all 29 resume templates are completely free with no signup or payment required. Vizonis Academy provides these as part of our mission to help students and professionals build successful careers in data and analytics.
What formats can I download the resumes in?
You can download each resume in both PDF format (for online applications) and Word/DOCX format (for editing). The Word format allows you to modify content, add your details, and personalize every section in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.
Which resume template should I use as a fresher?
As a fresher, choose templates labeled "Fresher" in your target role. These emphasize education, projects, internships, and skills rather than work experience. For example, use "Data Analyst Power BI & SQL — Fresher" if targeting a DA role.
Can I edit the downloaded Word resume?
Absolutely. The Word/DOCX download is fully editable in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or any compatible word processor. You can change names, add experience, modify skills, adjust formatting, and personalize every section.
What's the difference between Junior, Mid, and Senior templates?
Junior (0–3 years) focuses on learning, execution, and growing skills. Mid (4–5 years) highlights project ownership, mentoring, and measurable impact. Senior (5+ years) emphasizes leadership, strategic thinking, team management, and business transformation.
