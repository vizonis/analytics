Chapter 01

The ₹500 Crore Problem Nobody Talks About

Every business that sells on credit has the same nightmare: customers who don't pay on time. For a large Indian enterprise with ₹15,000Cr annual revenue, late payments don't just cause inconvenience — they destroy working capital, trigger borrowing costs, and in worst cases, lead to write-offs.

Here's what the math looks like for a typical large enterprise in India:

The Cost of Late Payments
Total receivables outstanding₹2,800 Cr
% that are overdue (>30 days)32%
Overdue amount₹896 Cr
Annual borrowing cost (10%)₹89.6 Cr
Bad debt write-offs (2%)₹17.9 Cr
Collection team cost (40 people)₹4.8 Cr
Disputed invoice resolution cost₹12.3 Cr
Total annual cost₹124.6 Cr
What Makes It Worse
  • Reactive, not proactive: Collections teams discover late payments after the due date has passed. By then, it's already expensive to recover.
  • No risk differentiation: All overdue invoices get the same treatment. A 1-day-late loyal customer gets the same reminder as a 60-day-late serial defaulter.
  • Data silos: Invoice data in ERP, payment history in bank statements, customer relationship in CRM, credit data in bureau — nobody sees the full picture.
  • Manual reporting: CFO gets AR aging report once a week. By the time they see a problem, it's already a crisis.
  • Generic collection tactics: Every overdue customer gets the same template email. No personalization, no intelligence, no strategy.

The Core Insight: The problem isn't that companies don't collect — it's that they collect blindly and reactively. The solution is a predictive analytics system that answers three questions for every invoice: Will it be paid late? How late? What's the best action to take right now? That's exactly what Microsoft Fabric + ML + Data Agent + Gen AI delivers. Chapter 04

The Machine Learning Model: Predicting Late Payments at 94% Accuracy

With unified data in Fabric's OneLake, the data science team built a classification model that predicts, for every open invoice, whether it will be paid on time, late (1-30 days), or very late (30+ days). Here's the full breakdown:

The 32 Features That Power the Prediction

The model doesn't just look at the invoice — it looks at the entire relationship between the company and the customer. Features are grouped into 6 categories:

Invoice Features (6)
  • • Invoice amount (log-transformed)
  • • Payment terms (Net 30/45/60/90)
  • • Days until due date
  • • Invoice type (standard, milestone, recurring)
  • • Number of line items
  • • Dispute flag (boolean)
Payment History Features (8)
  • • Average days to pay (last 12 invoices)
  • • Standard deviation of days to pay
  • • % of invoices paid late (rolling 6 months)
  • • Trend: getting better or worse?
  • • Longest overdue period ever
  • • Number of partial payments
  • • Average days between invoice and first payment
  • • Seasonal pattern detected (boolean)
Relationship Features (6)
  • • Customer tenure (months)
  • • Total revenue from customer (lifetime)
  • • Number of active invoices
  • • Ratio of disputed to total invoices
  • • Sales rep assigned (encoded)
  • • Customer segment (SMB/Mid/Enterprise)
External Features (6)
  • • Credit bureau score (latest)
  • • Credit score change (30-day delta)
  • • Industry sector risk index
  • • Customer's GST filing regularity
  • • Regional economic indicator
  • • Competitor default news (NLP on news feeds)
Interaction Features (4)
  • • Invoice acknowledgment status
  • • Number of reminder emails sent
  • • Email open rate (for this invoice)
  • • Last collection call outcome
Derived Features (2)
  • • Current outstanding balance / Annual revenue ratio
  • • Utilization of credit limit (%

Chapter 06

Generative AI: Automating Collection Strategy with Intelligence

Prediction tells you who will pay late. Data Agent tells you what's happening. But what do you actually do about it? This is where Generative AI transforms the collections process from generic spam into targeted, intelligent communication.

Three Ways Gen AI Supercharges Collections

1. AI-Drafted Collection Emails (Personalized at Scale)

GPT-4 generates unique emails based on customer profile, relationship history, and predicted risk

❌ OLD WAY: Generic Template
"Dear Customer, Your invoice #INV-24744 for ₹5,30,00,000 is overdue by 12 days. Please pay immediately to avoid late fees. Regards, Accounts Receivable Team."
✅ NEW WAY: Gen AI Personalized
"Hi Rajesh, I noticed invoice #24744 (₹5.3Cr) is due this week. I know JSW has been a great partner for 8 years and typically pays within 30 days — is there anything we should be aware of this time? Happy to discuss if the delivery on the Q3 batch had any issues. Best, Priya (your dedicated AR contact)"

Result: Open rate increased from 22% to 67%. Response rate increased from 8% to 41%. Collections from email alone went up 3x.

2. AI Collector Briefing (Before Every Call)

Gen AI creates a 1-page brief for collectors before they call each customer

🤖 AI Collector Brief — JSW Steel — Generated 2 min ago

Customer: JSW Steel Ltd | Relationship: 8 years | Annual revenue: ₹142Cr

Outstanding: 3 invoices, ₹12.4Cr total | Risk score: 89/100

Why they might be late: Credit score dropped 47pts. 3 disputes in 60 days. Steel sector stress. GST filing delayed.

Last interaction: Jan 8 — collector Anil called, customer said "waiting for internal approval." No follow-up since.

Recommended approach: DON'T mention credit score drop (sensitive). DO ask about the Q3 delivery dispute — that's likely the real blocker. Offer to split payment into 2 tranches if approval is the issue. Escalate to relationship manager if no commitment today.

Suggested opening: "Hi Rajesh, just checking in on those 3 invoices — I saw there was a delivery concern on the Q3 batch. Is that still unresolved on your end?"

3. AI Strategy Recommendations (Per Customer)

Gen AI suggests the optimal collection strategy based on predicted behavior

Low Risk (0-30)
Send automated friendly reminder 5 days before due date. No human intervention needed.
Medium Risk (31-70)
Send personalized email. If no response in 3 days, assign collector. Consider 1% early payment discount.
High Risk (71-100)
Immediate collector call with AI brief. Involve sales leadership. Consider credit hold on future orders. Legal review if 60+ days.

How does machine learning predict late payments in accounts receivable?

ML models analyze historical payment patterns, customer behavior, invoice details, economic indicators, and relationship data to calculate a 'late payment probability score' for each outstanding invoice. Features like days-to-pay history, dispute frequency, seasonal patterns, and credit utilization feed into classification models (XGBoost, Random Forest) that predict whether an invoice will be paid on time, late (1-30 days), or very late (30+ days). The model is retrained weekly with new payment data, so it continuously improves.

What is Microsoft Fabric Data Agent?

Microsoft Fabric Data Agent is an AI-powered conversational interface within Fabric that lets business users ask questions about their data in natural language and get instant answers with visualizations. For accounts receivable, a finance manager could ask 'Which customers are most likely to default this month?' and get a ranked list with risk scores — without writing a single SQL query. It uses Microsoft Copilot technology connected to your Fabric data.
