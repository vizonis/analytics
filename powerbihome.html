FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need any prior experience to join this Power BI course?

No. This course starts from absolute zero — "What is Power BI?" on Day 0. You don't need SQL, Python, or any programming background. If you know how to use Excel, that's a bonus but not required. The curriculum is designed for complete beginners and takes you to job-ready level in 8 days.

Is the training online or classroom?

Both. We offer weekend classroom batches in Bangalore (Marathahalli) and live online batches via Zoom. Online students get the exact same experience — screen sharing, doubt solving, hands-on labs. All sessions are recorded for lifetime access.

Will I get a certificate after completing the course?

Yes. You receive a Vizonis Academy completion certificate. But more importantly, you'll have a published Power BI portfolio with 3+ real dashboards that you can show in interviews — which carries 10x more weight than any certificate.

The course content on this website — is it free?

Yes. Every lesson on this website is 100% free to read — Day 0 through Day 8, all topics, all visual explanations. This is our commitment to open education. What you pay for in the classroom is: live instruction, hands-on labs with real datasets, personal doubt solving, resume building, mock interviews, and placement referrals.

What is the Power BI course fee in Bangalore?

We keep our pricing competitive and transparent. Chat with us on WhatsApp for the latest fee structure — we offer EMI options, early-bird discounts, and group discounts. Compared to institutes charging ₹25,000-40,000 for 3-month theory-heavy courses, our 8-day practical program delivers more value at a fraction of the cost.

Should I also get the Microsoft PL-300 certification?

Yes, we recommend it. Our course covers 95% of the PL-300 syllabus. After completing the 8-day training, you'll need 1-2 weeks of focused practice on Microsoft Learn to clear the exam. We guide you on exam strategy, practice questions, and registration. PL-300 adds credibility to your resume — but your portfolio matters more.
